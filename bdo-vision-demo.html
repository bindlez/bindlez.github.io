How the numbers work

Value = sum of captured quantity × example unit value.

Silver/hour = value ÷ observed seconds × 3,600. Rates appear after 60 observed seconds. Covered capture and manual pauses are excluded. This is item value, before material costs and fees.

In the Windows app, item IDs match Central Market prices or verified NPC values. Uncertain OCR is skipped; missing loot is never estimated. The browser demo uses a fixed, labeled sample catalog.
