Model & sources

Exact odds use the published Radiant skill weights, renormalized after excluding skill families learned in other slots. Locks restrict the planner's choices; they do not change the game's roll distribution. Free skill acquisition at levels 30, 40, and 50 is modeled as a zero-orb draw. Costs follow the current number of learned skills (2/3/4/5). The long-run forecast is a Monte Carlo estimate under the named strategy, not a proof of the globally optimal policy. The mean and percentiles are shown as unconditional only if all sampled runs finish before the simulation safety cap. Results should be checked against the in-game Change Skill probabilities if the game changes.
Sources: Pearl Abyss probability notice · Pearl Abyss fairy guide
