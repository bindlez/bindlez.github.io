What counts—and how the numbers work

Recorded value = Σ (captured quantity × market or verified NPC unit value). Silver/hour = recorded value ÷ observed seconds × 3,600.

Blocked, minimized, stale, unreadable and manually paused time is excluded. Readable periods with no drops still count. Unknown prices are excluded from a labeled partial total. Unlisted items use a published NPC sell value only when the item ID and name match; otherwise they stay unpriced. Material costs and fees are not deducted.
